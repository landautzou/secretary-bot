Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Secretary Bot is a personal student project. It runs on its owner's laptop whenever that laptop is on, not on a company server, and is used by the owner and a few friends. This page explains what it reads, what it keeps, who else sees any of it, and how to delete it.

What the bot reads

  • Discord: your Discord user ID and the direct messages and button taps you send the bot, so it can answer you.
  • Calendar links you give it: if you use !setup, the Canvas calendar feed link and Google Calendar secret iCal links you paste in. The bot uses them only to download your calendar and build your lists.
  • Google Calendar, if you sign in: if you sign in with !google, the bot can add, move and delete events on your Google Calendar, only when you ask it to.
  • Google Tasks (owner only): the bot's owner can also let it see and edit their Google Tasks: it reads task lists to show what is due, and marks a task complete when it is checked off.
  • Keys and settings you give it: with !keys, your own Groq key and where you usually leave from, for bus directions.

What the bot keeps, and where

Everything is stored in files on the owner's laptop. The owner can open these files, so only share links and keys with an owner you trust.

  • Your calendar links, Groq key, home place, and Google sign-in (if you gave them).
  • Which items you checked off and which quizzes you marked booked (kept for 60 days), reminders you set (until they are sent), and which warnings and daily messages you were sent, so none is sent twice.
  • The secret part of your private dashboard link.
  • If you turn on phone notifications in the dashboard: for each device, the address its push service gave it and the keys to send to it, until you turn them off.
  • From the dashboard's Tasks tab: a copy of the name, place and due date of a Google Calendar task you check off there (its event is deleted, so Undo can put it back), and how long a task took when you answer "How long did it take?", with the kind of task (such as "Stat 200, R HW"), kept for 120 days to estimate similar tasks.
  • A copy of the messages the bot sends you on its own (such as the morning message and reminders), and what you type to it in the dashboard's Chat and its answers, so the dashboard can show them. The newest 300 of each are kept.
  • An activity log of what the bot did for each person, such as "setup saved" or "reminder set", with the time. It never holds what you typed, your links, keys or Discord ID: people are tagged with a code that can't be turned back into who they are without a secret that stays on the laptop.
  • A technical log of what the bot did and any errors, used to fix problems. It is replaced as it grows.

Apart from those task copies, calendar events and tasks are downloaded fresh each time and are not saved. On your own phone or computer, the dashboard page remembers your private link and the last plan it loaded, so it can still show it when the bot is off; clearing that site's data in your browser removes them.

Who else sees any of it

To do some things, the bot sends only what is needed to these services, whose own privacy policies apply:

  • Groq (the AI that understands normal messages): the text of messages you type that aren't ! commands. If you added your own Groq key, it also gets your upcoming calendar items to answer questions like "what do I have tomorrow?". Without your own key, the owner's key is used for your message text only, never your calendar.
  • OpenStreetMap: place names, such as an event's location, to find the address.
  • Champaign-Urbana MTD: map positions of the places in a trip, to find buses.
  • Google: requests to your own Google Calendar and Tasks, when you have signed in.
  • Your device's push service (Apple's for an iPhone, Google's, Microsoft's or Mozilla's for other browsers), if you turn on phone notifications: the first line of each message the bot sends you on its own, encrypted so only your device can read it.
  • The tunnel service that carries the dashboard to your phone: your dashboard page passes through it on the way.

What the bot never does

  • It does not sell, rent or share your data beyond the list above.
  • It does not show ads or use analytics or tracking.
  • It does not use your data for anything except making your own lists, reminders and answers.

Secretary Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Send the bot !forget to delete everything it keeps about you: your calendar links, keys, Google sign-in, dashboard link, check-offs, quiz bookings, reminders, phone notification settings, copies of checked-off tasks and how long tasks took, the record and copies of messages it sent you, your dashboard chat, and your activity history.
  • To make sure an old calendar link can't be used by anyone, reset it in Canvas or Google Calendar.
  • To remove the bot's Google access, open your Google account's third-party access page and remove Secretary Bot.
  • To remove a Groq key you gave it, send !keys and type "remove", or delete the key at console.groq.com.

Questions

Message the bot's owner on Discord, or email the support address shown on the Google sign-in screen.
