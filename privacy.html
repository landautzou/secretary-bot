Privacy policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Secretary Bot is a personal student project. It runs on its owner's laptop, not on a company server, and is used by the owner and a few friends. This page explains what it reads, what it keeps, and how to delete it.

What the bot reads

  • Discord: your Discord user ID and the direct messages and button taps you send the bot, so it can answer you.
  • Calendar links you give it: if you use !setup, the Canvas calendar feed link and Google Calendar secret iCal links you paste in. The bot uses them only to download your calendar and build your lists.
  • Google Tasks (owner only): when the bot's owner signs in with Google, the bot can see and edit their Google Tasks. It reads task lists and tasks to show what is due, and marks a task complete when it is checked off in Discord. Friends never sign in with Google.

What the bot keeps, and where

Everything is stored in files on the owner's laptop: calendar links that friends set up, which items you checked off and which quizzes you marked booked (kept for 60 days), and the owner's Google sign-in. The owner can open these files, so only share links with an owner you trust. Calendar events and tasks are downloaded fresh each time and are not saved.

What the bot never does

  • It does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not show ads or use analytics or tracking.
  • It does not use your data for anything except making your own lists and reminders.

Secretary Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Send the bot !forget to delete your calendar links, check-offs and quiz bookings.
  • To make sure an old calendar link can't be used by anyone, reset it in Canvas or Google Calendar.
  • To remove the bot's Google access, open your Google account's third-party access page and remove Secretary Bot.

Questions

Message the bot's owner on Discord, or email the support address shown on the Google sign-in screen.
