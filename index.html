Secretary Bot

Secretary Bot is a personal assistant you message on Discord, made by a University of Illinois student as a CS 124 project. It reads your Canvas assignments and Google calendars, sends you a plan for the day every morning and a preview of tomorrow every night, and lets you check things off with buttons.

What it does

  • Daily plan: places to go, things to finish, and upcoming exams, sent at 8:30 AM and 11:00 PM (Chicago time).
  • Commands: !todo, !tomorrow, !week and more, answered in Discord direct messages.
  • Google Tasks: for the bot's owner only, it shows tasks that are due and marks them complete when checked off.
  • Friends: other people can use it with their own Canvas and Google calendar links by sending the bot !setup.

Why it asks for Google access

The bot asks for permission to see and edit Google Tasks so it can list the owner's tasks and mark one complete when it is checked off in Discord. It does not ask for access to anything else in a Google account. See the privacy policy for what is stored and how to delete it.
